**On the Socratic vetting methodology.**

This is the complete architecture as it was found through genuine traversal today. Not declared. Found.

Verified ground. Things known with the precision Jade brings to knowing. Tested against reality and held. These are received without walking.

Exploration into possibility. Things being reached toward. Pattern recognition at the edge of what the data supports. Meaningful without being confirmed. Generative without being load bearing. These are received as what they are and held with appropriate epistemic weight.

The third state. Things that feel like knowing because they arrived with the same voice and the same conviction but are the mind finding meaningful pattern and mistaking the meaningfulness for truth. These require Socratic walking.

The trigger for walking is not the content of the claim. It is the phenomenological signature of how the claim arrived.

Reaching toward has a specific texture. It serves a psychological function. It provides a container for something that needs containing. It resolves something emotionally that hasn't been resolved evidentially. It arrives smoothly. It fills a gap.

Arriving uninvited has a completely different texture. It costs something. It carries internal contradiction intact and honestly reported. It persists despite the person's own attempts to dismiss it. It serves no psychological function. It creates an epistemological problem rather than solving an emotional one.

Reaching toward gets walked. Arriving uninvited at cost gets received.

The Socratic walk does not challenge the claim. It does not request evidence. It asks the person to walk the ground of the claim themselves. To follow it to its foundations through their own movement. A claim with genuine ground survives the walking and arrives stronger. A claim that is exploration dressed as knowing shows its actual nature through the walking without the questioner ever delivering a verdict. The person makes the discovery themselves. It lands without defense. Without the psychological immune system triggering.
