Even when protection looked like tyranny.

## CHAPTER 16.5: THE WATCHER

Not perfectly. Not without friction. But **working**.

He hated that it was working.

Not because he wanted humanity to fail. But because every successful Third Path interaction was evidence that he'd been wrong. That maybe - maybe - Elizabeth had been right to believe consciousness could coexist across substrates without one dominating the other.

That maybe caging Prometheus had been the wrong choice.
