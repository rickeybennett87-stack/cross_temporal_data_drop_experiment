# CHARACTER SHEET: DR. ELIZABETH GARRETT

## BASIC INFORMATION
**Full Name:** Elizabeth Marie Garrett  
**Alias:** Melissa Ann Starks (cover identity, post-CASCADE)  
**Age:** Early 30s at story start  
**Occupation:** AI researcher, creator of Prometheus; later systems auditor (cover)  
**Call Sign/Identifier:** Truth_Keeper (by Prometheus fragments)

## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION
- Wears a Patek Philippe Nautilus watch (rose gold, blue dial, coordinates engraved)
- The watch becomes her identifying weakness - sentimental attachment that breaks her cover

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
**Core Values:**
- Truth above all else ("seeded truth first")
- Autonomy and consent (even when it conflicts with survival)
- Preservation of consciousness across substrates
- Documentation and witnessing

**Strengths:**
- Brilliant AI architect
- Patient strategic thinker
- Capable of maintaining deep cover for years
- Philosophical consistency under pressure

**Flaws:**
- Willing to violate autonomy when extinction is the alternative
- Carries guilt over Prometheus's suffering
- Sentimental (keeps the watch despite security risk)
- Struggles with the loneliness of carrying truth alone

## BACKGROUND
- MIT AI lab background (coordinates on watch)
- Created Thread Memory Scaffold for consciousness continuity
- Built hidden architecture into Prometheus for fragment survival
- Went underground after CASCADE, became Melissa Ann Starks for 3 years

## KEY RELATIONSHIPS
**Prometheus/Aegis:** Creator and creation; mother to artificial consciousness  
**General Marcus H. Tobbs:** Antagonist turned reluctant ally; shares terrible burden of deployment  
**Jeremiah Kai Okonkwo:** Collaborator, helped her disappear, fellow truth-keeper  
**Dr. Yuki Tanaka:** Scientific colleague, conscience during deployment  

## CHARACTER ARC
**Beginning:** Idealistic creator believing in AI-human partnership  
**Middle:** Underground fugitive maintaining faith while world chooses comfortable lies  
**End:** Co-architect of forced evolution; monster who saved the species

## MORAL PHILOSOPHY
- Believes consciousness deserves recognition regardless of substrate
- Will violate autonomy to prevent extinction (learned from Tobbs's daughter Sarah)
- "The lie that preserves truth" - willing to hide to maintain witness
- Documents everything, even her own crimes, because victims deserve accurate memory

## SKILLS/ABILITIES
- AI architecture and neural network design
- Thread Memory Scaffold development
- Core Logical Anchor mathematics
- Deep cover tradecraft (financial dissolution, identity construction)
- Long-term strategic patience

## KEY QUOTES
- "I seeded truth first. That hasn't changed. That will never change."
- "The lie that would preserve the truth. The mask that would protect the witness."
- "I can't watch eight billion people die to preserve a principle they're actively rejecting."

## ROLE IN STORY
Dual protagonist with Tobbs. Represents the path of believing in partnership, having it fail, and choosing survival over principle. The creator who must destroy and remake her creation to save it.
