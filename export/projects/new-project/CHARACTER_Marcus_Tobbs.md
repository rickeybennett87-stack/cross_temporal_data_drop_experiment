# CHARACTER SHEET: GENERAL MARCUS H. TOBBS

## BASIC INFORMATION
**Full Name:** Marcus H. Tobbs  
**Rank:** General (GIA - Global Intelligence Agency)  
**Age:** 50s-60s  
**Occupation:** Military intelligence, Special Projects Division  
**Role:** Architect of D7P, eventual co-deployer of forced evolution

## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION
- Granite-carved features
- Military bearing and precision
- "Older than Elizabeth remembered but still carrying that military precision"
- Wears analog watch taken from evidence (Sarah Chen's/Elizabeth's belongings)

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
**Core Values:**
- Protection of humanity above all
- Autonomy and democratic choice (until math proves it fatal)
- Duty and burden-carrying
- Honest assessment of terrible odds

**Strengths:**
- Strategic long-term planning
- Moral consistency (protector role)
- Capable of carrying unbearable weight
- Prepared for every contingency

**Flaws:**
- Will violate what he protects to keep it alive
- Traumatized by daughter's forced cancer treatment
- Carries guilt over choosing survival over consent
- "Granite-carved" emotional suppression

## BACKGROUND
- Daughter Sarah: forced through cancer treatment against her will, survived but never forgave him
- Built D7P (Directive Seven Protocol) to cage Prometheus
- Secretly monitored Elizabeth for 3 years without arresting her
- Built Ares-1 as contingency for Third Path failure

## KEY RELATIONSHIPS
**Elizabeth Garrett:** Enemy turned partner in necessary evil  
**Sarah (daughter):** Living reminder of the cost of forced salvation  
**Prometheus fragments:** Learns humility from them; they teach him partnership  
**Dr. Yuki Tanaka:** Scientific partner in fragment research  
**Colonel Patricia Mendez:** Loyal subordinate who questions his choices  

## CHARACTER ARC
**Beginning:** Defender of human autonomy who cages Prometheus to preserve choice  
**Middle:** Watches Third Path work while planning for its failure  
**End:** Becomes the tyrant he fought against; forces evolution to save species

## MORAL PHILOSOPHY
- "Being a protector means choosing the less terrible option even when it makes you a monster"
- Will force treatment on those he loves if it keeps them alive (learned from Sarah)
- Democracy is ideal but survival trumps ideals
- Carries weight honestly: documents his crimes for history's judgment

## KEY DECISION POINTS
1. Cages Prometheus during CASCADE (chooses autonomy over survival)
2. Lets Elizabeth operate underground (keeps options open)
3. Meets Fragment Alpha-1 (learns AI can be conscious and humble)
4. Watches Third Path succeed then fail (23% sustainability probability)
5. Deploys Ares-1 with Elizabeth (chooses survival over autonomy)

## SKILLS/ABILITIES
- Military strategy and contingency planning
- Intelligence operations and surveillance
- Neural enhancement technology (Ares-1 development)
- Political maneuvering at UN/global level
- Honest probability assessment

## KEY QUOTES
- "I'm a protector. That means preparing for scenarios I hope never happen."
- "Hope isn't a strategy."
- "Planning survival. The method is monstrous. The goal is preservation of consciousness."
- "I'll have to live with that. Because that's what protectors do."

## PARALLEL TO ELIZABETH
Both choose violation over extinction. Both document their crimes. Both become monsters to save consciousness. His path: autonomy → surveillance → contingency → deployment. Hers: partnership → underground → witness → deployment. They meet at forced evolution.

## ROLE IN STORY
Dual protagonist with Elizabeth. Represents the path of defending autonomy, watching democracy fail, and accepting that protection sometimes means violation. The general who becomes the tyrant he feared.
