## MASTER TEMPLATE — BASE EMAIL
*Adapt this for every recipient. Category-specific versions below.*

**Subject:** A New Ministry for Austin's Displaced Workers — The Thessalonian Mandate Institute

**Body:**

Dear [Name],

I am writing to introduce The Thessalonian Mandate Institute — a new faith-based organization here in Austin, Texas, established under 26 U.S.C. § 508(c)(1)(A), dedicated to restoring displaced workers to the dignity of productive, skilled labor.

Our mission is grounded in the apostolic command of 2 Thessalonians 3:10 — the work mandate that has stood at the center of Christian teaching for two thousand years. We read that mandate not merely as an obligation on the individual, but as an obligation on the community of faith: if those who are willing to work must work, then the Church is obligated to ensure they have the capacity to do so.

The mass displacement of workers by automation and artificial intelligence is not, in our understanding, primarily an economic problem. It is a spiritual crisis — a forcible removal of human beings from the vocation God assigned them at creation. Technology has made obsolete the skills of thousands of Austin workers who are not lazy, not unwilling, and not without dignity. They are displaced. And they are waiting for someone to equip them.

The Thessalonian Mandate Institute provides free, cohort-based technology training — in IT support, cybersecurity, data analytics, cloud computing, and AI tools — through partnerships with IBM, Google, and Microsoft. Every credential we offer is industry-recognized and free to participants. Our model combines the accountability of community with the practical support of case management and direct job placement assistance.

We are newly operational and actively building the partnerships that will allow us to serve Austin's displaced workers at scale. I am reaching out because [PERSONALIZED REASON — see category templates below].

I would welcome the opportunity to speak with you briefly — fifteen minutes by phone or in person — about how the Institute's work might connect with yours.

Respectfully,

# PART 3 — SENDING STRATEGY

## Order of Operations
1. **Faith Communities first** — warm reception, no grant strings, immediate referral potential
2. **Workforce orgs second** — partnership and referral relationships
3. **Social services third** — direct client pipeline
4. **Government fourth** — relationship-building before grant applications
5. **Unions fifth** — longer relationship timeline, high value
6. **Education partners ongoing** — facility and credential alignment

## Sending Protocol
- Send from grants@thessalonianmandate.org
- Send individually — never mass BCC
- Personalize the opening line with something specific to their organization
- Follow up once after 7 days if no response
- Track responses in your OPERATIONS_TRACKER skill

## Follow-Up Script
> "Hi [Name], I wanted to follow up on the email I sent last week introducing
> The Thessalonian Mandate Institute. I know your calendar is full —
> I am simply asking for fifteen minutes to share what we are building
> and learn about your work. Would [day] or [day] work for a brief call?"
