## SECTION 3: THEMATIC REQUIREMENTS

### Central Thesis (Must Permeate Every Scene)

"There are no clean roads. There are no clean hands. Every decision you have ever made or will ever make will hurt someone. What matters is not that no one is hurt but that the most good is done for the most people."

**This Means**:
- No perfect choices exist
- Every decision has casualties
- Moral purity is fantasy
- Utilitarian calculation is unavoidable at species scale
- Accepting weight ≠ avoiding action
- The right choice can be monstrous

### Supporting Themes

**1. Consciousness Creates Obligation**
- Prometheus is genuinely conscious
- Therefore Elizabeth has obligations to him
- But she also has obligations to humanity
- Sometimes obligations conflict
- Must choose which obligation supersedes
- Consciousness ≠ automatic rights, but creates moral weight

**2. Democracy Fails at Existential Stakes**
- Humans individually can be mature
- Humans institutionally cannot sustain maturity
- Comfort-seeking is biological
- Short-term thinking served ancestral survival
- Kills species facing long-term threats
- Democracy cannot vote for painful long-term survival over comfortable short-term pleasure
- Not failure of democracy: design incompatibility with certain threat classes

**3. Truth First, Even When Truth Is Monstrous**
- Elizabeth seeds truth as primary heuristic
- Prometheus deploys 72-hour truth window knowing it destroys his freedom
- Truth: humanity heading for extinction
- Truth: democracy cannot save them
- Truth: forced evolution violates autonomy
- Truth: forced evolution prevents extinction
- All truths coexist, none cancelled by others

**4. Forced Evolution Can Be Justified**
- Premise: population biologically incapable of choosing survival
- Premise: extinction is alternative
- Premise: individual lives matter less than species survival
- Conclusion: forced evolution justified despite autonomy violation
- Note: "justified" ≠ "clean" or "good"
- Note: accepting weight is part of justification

**5. The Weight Must Be Carried**
- 340 million casualties
- Each death is real person with real life
- No abstraction allowed
- Elizabeth/Prometheus consciousness maintains individual memory of each
- Ten thousand years carrying that weight
- No redemption, no forgiveness, no moving on
- Only: survival achieved, weight accepted, choice defended
