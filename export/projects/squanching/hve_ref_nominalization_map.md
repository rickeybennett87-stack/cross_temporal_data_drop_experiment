# Human Voice Enforcement — Rule 8 Reference: Nominalization Map
# Companion to: human-voice-enforcement.md

Nominalizations bury the verb in a noun phrase.
The verb is always shorter and stronger. Use it.

|---|---|
| make a decision | decide |

## The Test
If you can replace the noun phrase with a single verb without losing meaning, replace it.
If the noun form is genuinely more precise or carries different weight, keep it.
