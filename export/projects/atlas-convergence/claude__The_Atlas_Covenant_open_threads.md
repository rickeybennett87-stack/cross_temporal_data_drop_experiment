# The Atlas Covenant — Open Threads (planted, not yet paid off)

Tracked here so a later chapter doesn't have to be reconstructed from memory. Check this doc before writing any chapter that could resolve one of these.

## Untranslated burst transmission (planted chapter 5)

During the Gek ambush in chapter 5, the unknown vessel (deliberately not identified as Gek in the prose during the engagement itself — the crew has no confirmed identification in the moment, only sees "the unknown vessel" / "the enemy") sends a compressed burst transmission in the same instant as its first strike. Anke flags it as encoded in an architecture the Constantine's translation library doesn't recognize, has no time to crack it live, and logs it raw to the ship's recorder. Tavik orders it logged and moved past, since it arrived after weapons fire had already started.

This transmission is deliberately left untranslated in chapter 5. At some later point in the story, it must be translated on the page, and the translated text is fixed as:

"Power down drives and submit to the Gek First Spawn. Prepare to be boarded. Or you will be destroyed."

This is a specific instruction from the author, not an authorial invention to be altered — the exact wording above must be used verbatim when the translation scene is written. Whichever chapter handles this should build toward it deliberately (who translates it, why now, and what it changes for a crew that will realize, after the fact, that the enemy tried to issue an ultimatum before boarding rather than attacking on pure sight). Do not have any character definitively call the ship "Gek" before this translation lands — identification stays deferred until this payoff.

## Style/identification convention going forward

Any hostile-contact scene where identity isn't yet confirmed to the point-of-view characters should use neutral terms ("the unknown vessel," "the enemy," "the contact") rather than naming the species outright, reserving named identification for the moment the story actually earns it in-scene.
