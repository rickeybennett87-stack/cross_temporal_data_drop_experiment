---

### ⚖️ Core Requirements
- **Accuracy:**  
  - The transcript must faithfully capture the words spoken or written, without alteration or omission.  
  - Errors, paraphrasing, or editorializing can undermine admissibility.

- **Authenticity:**  
  - There must be a reliable method of proving that the transcript is genuine.  
  - This often involves metadata, timestamps, stable identifiers, or certification by a neutral party (e.g., court reporter, notary).

- **Chain of Custody:**  
  - The transcript must be preserved in a way that prevents tampering.  
  - Documentation of who created, stored, and accessed the transcript is critical.

- **Attribution:**  
  - Each statement must be clearly attributed to the correct speaker.  
  - Ambiguity about who said what can make the transcript inadmissible.

- **Compliance with Rules of Evidence:**  
  - The transcript must avoid hearsay unless it falls under a recognized exception.  
  - It must be relevant to the case and not unfairly prejudicial.

---
