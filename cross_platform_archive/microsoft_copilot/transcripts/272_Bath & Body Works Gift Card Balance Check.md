Terpenes are nature’s aromatic architects 🌿—organic compounds found in many plants (and some animals) that give them their distinctive scents, flavors, and even colors. Think of the citrusy zing in an orange peel, the calming scent of lavender, or the piney sharpness of a forest breeze—that’s terpenes at work.

### 🧬 What They Do
- **In plants**: Terpenes help attract pollinators, repel predators, and protect against environmental stress.
- **In humans**: They may interact with our brain and body in subtle ways. Some are believed to have calming, energizing, or anti-inflammatory effects, though research is still unfolding.

### 🌿 In Cannabis
Terpenes are especially famous in cannabis, where they contribute to the “entourage effect”—the idea that terpenes and cannabinoids (like THC and CBD) work together to shape the plant’s effects. That’s why two strains with the same THC level can feel totally different.

### 🧪 Examples
- **Limonene**: Found in citrus, may elevate mood.
- **Myrcene**: Earthy and musky, often sedating.
- **Pinene**: Pine-scented, may aid alertness.
