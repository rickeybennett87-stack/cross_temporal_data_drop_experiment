## 🍎 Classic Mulled Apple Cider Recipe

### Ingredients
- 2 quarts apple cider  
- ½ cup brown sugar  
- 1 teaspoon whole allspice  
- 1 teaspoon whole cloves  
- ¼ teaspoon salt  
- Dash of nutmeg  
- 3 cinnamon sticks  
- Orange wedges studded with cloves (for garnish)

### Instructions
1. **Combine** cider, sugar, and spices in a large saucepan.  
2. **Bring to a boil**, then reduce heat.  
3. **Cover and simmer** for 20 minutes to let the flavors infuse.  
4. **Strain** out the spices.  
5. **Serve hot** in mugs, garnished with clove-studded orange wedges.

---

## 🍊 Variations You Might Like
- **Slow Cooker Method:** Add 1 gallon unfiltered apple juice, an orange, ginger, cinnamon sticks, and cloves. Cook on low for 4 hours.  
- **Festive Upgrade:** Add cranberries, sliced apples, and a splash of brandy for a holiday twist.  
- **Quick Version:** Simmer cider with cinnamon, cloves, and orange slices for just 10 minutes.

---

## ✨ Tips for Best Flavor
- Use **fresh, unfiltered apple cider** for depth.  
- Whole spices give a **clearer, smoother flavor** than ground.  
- Adjust sweetness with honey, maple syrup, or extra sugar depending on taste.  
- For gatherings, keep it warm in a **slow cooker** so guests can serve themselves.
