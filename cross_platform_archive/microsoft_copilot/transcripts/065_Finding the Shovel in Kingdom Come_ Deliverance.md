## 🪓 Where to Find the Shovel to Bury Henry’s Parents  
In **Kingdom Come: Deliverance**, during the *Homecoming* quest, the shovel you need is found **right in Skalitz**, and the game guides you there through a very specific trigger.

### 🎯 Exact Location & How to Get It
- After Henry finds his parents’ bodies, start walking **back toward the center of Skalitz**.
- You’ll soon hear **a dog barking** — that’s your signal.  
- Follow the barking to find **Mutt**, the butcher’s dog, confronting **Zbyshek**, a looter.  
- Zbyshek has the **spade** you need.  
  - You can **talk to him**,  
  - **Fight him**, or  
  - Let him **run away**.  
- After the encounter, the **shovel will be stuck in the ground nearby**. Just pick it up.
