### 🧪 Step-by-Step Guide to Access the Anteverse and Get Anteverse Gel

#### 🔓 1. **Access the Containment Area**
- Look for the **open cell door with yellow paint**—this marks the path toward the Helmholtz Control zone.
- You’ll encounter **turrets** guarding the area. Don’t rush in—you’ll be gunned down.

#### 🛠️ 2. **Disable the Turrets**
- Safest method: **Build a bridge** across the live fire zone.
- Progress through the story until you can **reroute the turrets** to fire at marines instead of you.

#### 👾 3. **Defeat the Tarasque**
- This massive creature guards the portal to the Anteverse.
- Use **Leyak essences** (from red tentacled monsters) to pass through the **X-Ray gate**.
- Fight the Tarasque from a safe distance—bait it downstairs and shoot from behind a gate or elevated platform.

#### 🌌 4. **Enter the Mushroom Portal World**
- Once the Tarasque is defeated, the **Far Garden (Mycofields)** portal opens.
- This is the **Anteverse** zone where you can farm **Anteverse Gel**.

#### 💡 Pro Tips
- Bring **X-Ray Lamps or Towers** to farm Leyak essences.
- Equip strong weapons like **Electro-Throwers**.
- A **Tier 3 Keypad Hacker** may be required for certain doors.
- Don’t forget **flashlights**—the portal world is dark and eerie.
