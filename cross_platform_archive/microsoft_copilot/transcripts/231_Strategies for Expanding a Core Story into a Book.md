## 🗣️ Verbal Prompting (VS Method) in ABA

- **Definition**: A verbal prompt is a spoken cue or instruction that helps a learner respond correctly. It’s one of the least intrusive forms of prompting and is often used early in skill acquisition.
- **Example**: If teaching a child to say “hello,” a verbal prompt might be “Can you say hello?”
- **Hierarchy**: Verbal prompts are part of a broader **prompt hierarchy**, which includes:
  - Physical prompts (most intrusive)
  - Visual prompts
  - Verbal prompts
  - Gestural prompts
  - Independent responding (goal)

- **Goal**: Prompts are meant to be **faded over time** so the learner becomes independent. Verbal prompts are often faded by reducing the specificity or volume of the cue.

You can explore more on [Master ABA’s guide to prompt hierarchy](https://masteraba.com/prompt-hierarchy/) or [Rori’s breakdown of verbal prompt elements](https://www.rori.care/post/7-key-elements-of-verbal-prompt-hierarchy-aba-for-effective-learning).
