- **Carry-on limit:** 2 personal items (up to 25 lbs each) and 2 carry-on bags (up to 50 lbs each).

---

### 🚫 Prohibited Items
Amtrak bans certain items in both carry-on and checked baggage:
- **Weapons:** Firearms, explosives, and knives (except pocket knives under 3 inches).
- **Flammable materials:** Gasoline, lighter fluid, fireworks.
- **Pressurized containers:** Including propane tanks and aerosol sprays.
- **Animals:** Comfort animals are *not* allowed unless certified as service animals. Pets are allowed on select routes with a ticket.
- **Other items:** Archery equipment (only in checked baggage), acid-based batteries, and recreational drugs.

---

### 🐾 Pets & Service Animals
- **Pets:** Allowed on select trains for trips under 7 hours. Must be in carriers and weigh under 20 lbs.

- **Tickets must match ID name** exactly.

---

### 🚆 Onboard Conduct
