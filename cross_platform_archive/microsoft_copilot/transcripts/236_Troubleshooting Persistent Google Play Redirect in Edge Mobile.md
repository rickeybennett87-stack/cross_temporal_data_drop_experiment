### 🔍 Why It Happens
Many users report that Edge Mobile prompts to open Google Play due to:
- A **previously allowed site** with permission to launch external apps
- A **malicious redirect** embedded in a homepage or startup tab
- A **cached service worker or extension residue** from a migrated Chrome profile

---

### 🛠️ What You Can Do

#### 1. **Check Site Permissions**
Go to:
**Edge Settings → Site Permissions → All Sites**  
Look for any site with permission to “Open external apps” and revoke it.

#### 2. **Clear Startup Behavior**
Navigate to:
**Settings → On Startup → Open a new tab**  
Avoid “Continue where you left off” or any custom URL that might be triggering the redirect.

#### 3. **Disable External App Launch**
Ensure this toggle is off:
**Settings → Privacy and Security → Block external app launches**  
Even if it’s already off, toggle it off/on again to reset the flag.

#### 4. **Remote Debugging (Advanced)**
If you want to trace the exact source:
- Connect your Android device to a PC via USB
- Enable **Developer Mode** on your phone
- Use [Edge’s Remote Debugging Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/devtools/remote-debugging/) to inspect live traffic and redirects

---

### 🧼 Bonus: Clean the Cache
Sometimes the culprit is a rogue service worker or cached redirect:
- Go to **Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data**
- Select **Cookies, Cached images/files, Site settings**
