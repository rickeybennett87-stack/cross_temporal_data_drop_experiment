### 📱 Wallpaper Engine on Apple Devices
- **iOS (iPhone/iPad):** Wallpaper Engine is **not supported** on iOS. Apple restricts background processes and GPU-level access, which prevents the app from running natively.  
- **macOS (Mac computers):** Similarly, Wallpaper Engine does **not exist for macOS**. The developers have stated it’s not economically viable to port it due to low demand compared to Windows.
