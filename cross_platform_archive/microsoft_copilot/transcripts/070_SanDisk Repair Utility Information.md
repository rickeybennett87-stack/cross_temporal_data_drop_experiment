## ✅ **The Official SanDisk Utility: SanDisk SecureErase**
This is the only true SanDisk‑authored tool designed to *fix* a drive in the sense of restoring it to factory state. It securely wipes supported SanDisk/WD SSDs and resets their internal mapping tables.  


### What it does
- Performs a full **Sanitize/Secure Erase**  
- Restores SSD performance by clearing all written blocks  
- Works through a **bootable USB environment**  
- Integrated into the **SanDisk Dashboard** (Windows)

### Where to get it
SanDisk distributes SecureErase through the **SanDisk Dashboard** download page:  


The Dashboard includes:
- Firmware updater  
- Drive analysis  
- SecureErase launcher

## ⚠️ Important Clarification
There is **no official SanDisk repair tool** for:
- USB flash drives  
- SD/microSD cards  
- External portable SSDs  

For those, SanDisk recommends OS‑level tools (CHKDSK, DiskPart, Disk Utility) or third‑party recovery utilities — but those are *not* official.
