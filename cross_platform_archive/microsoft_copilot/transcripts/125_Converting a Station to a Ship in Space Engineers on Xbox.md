**Quick Answer:** On Xbox in *Space Engineers*, you can convert a station into a ship without rebuilding by enabling the **“Allow Convert to Station”** setting in your world options, then using the **Control Panel** of a cockpit or remote control block to select **“Convert to Ship”**. If the option doesn’t appear, it usually means your grid is touching voxels (like an asteroid or planet surface), so you’ll need to detach it or use admin tools to move it free.

---

### 🔧 Step-by-Step Guide (Xbox Controller)
1. **Check World Settings**
   - From the main menu, go to **Load Game → Edit Settings → Advanced Settings → Misc**.
   - Make sure **“Allow Convert to Station”** is enabled. Without this, the option won’t show up.

2. **Add a Control Block**
   - Place a **cockpit**, **remote control**, or **flight seat** on your grid.
   - Sit in the cockpit or access the block’s **Control Panel**.

3. **Find the Convert Option**
   - In the Control Panel, look for **“Convert to Ship”**.
   - If available, select it. Your station will now behave like a ship, allowing thrusters, gyros, and moving parts to function.

4. **If the Option Doesn’t Appear**
   - **Detach from voxels**: Even a tiny piece of asteroid or planet surface touching your grid will force it to remain a station. Use drills or voxel hands (if admin tools are enabled) to clear space around it.
   - **Disconnect connectors/merge blocks**: If your grid is linked to another station, remove those links.
   - **Creative workaround**: Save the grid as a **blueprint**, then respawn/paste it in open space. This forces it to be a ship.

---

### ⚠️ Common Pitfalls
- **Buried builds**: If part of your grid is inside terrain, forcing it to convert can cause glitches or explosions.
- **Survival mode limits**: Without admin tools, you’ll need to physically grind away voxels or rebuild small sections.
- **Unsupported Stations setting**: If enabled, stations not attached to voxels may automatically convert to ships, which can be confusing.

---

### 🎮 Controller Tip
On Xbox, the **Control Panel** is accessed by:
- Sitting in a cockpit/seat → Press **X** to open terminal.
- Navigate with the D-pad or joystick to find the **Convert to Ship** option.

---

**In short:** Place a cockpit, check your settings, and make sure the grid isn’t touching voxels. If the option still doesn’t appear, use creative/admin tools to move or blueprint the grid into free space. That way, you won’t have to rebuild everything from scratch.

### 🧩 Troubleshooting Why “Convert to Ship” Isn’t Available

#### 1. **Check Grid Type**
- Go to the **Control Panel** of any block on the grid (like a cockpit or terminal).
- Look at the **“Info” tab**.
- If it says **“Static Grid”**, it’s still considered a station.
- If it says **“Large Ship”** or **“Small Ship”**, it’s already a ship.

If it’s a **Static Grid** and the “Convert to Ship” button is missing, continue below.

---

#### 2. **Make Sure It’s a Single Grid**
- If your station is connected to another grid via **merge blocks**, **connectors**, or **landing gear**, it may block conversion.
- Try disconnecting any merge blocks or connectors and recheck the option.

---

#### 3. **Voxel Contact Can Be Invisible**
- Even if it looks like it’s floating, a single block touching terrain can prevent conversion.
- Use a **drill** or **creative mode voxel hand** to clear space around the base.
- Try nudging the grid slightly with a **piston**, **rotor**, or **explosive block** to break contact.

- Go into **creative mode** (or enable admin tools).
- Aim at the grid and press **D-Pad Up** to create a **blueprint**.
- Open the **blueprint menu** and **paste it into open space**.
- The pasted version will be a ship by default.
