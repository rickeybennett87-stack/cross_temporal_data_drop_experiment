### 🎮 Where to Find Your Xbox Play History

- **Xbox Console (Series X|S or One)**
  - Go to your **Profile & System** menu.
  - Select **My Profile** → **Gaming** → **Activity Feed**.
  - Scroll through your **“Recently Played”** list. This shows every game you’ve launched, even years back.

- **Xbox App (PC or Mobile)**
  - Open the **Xbox app** and sign in with your account.
  - Tap your **profile icon** → **Games**.
  - You’ll see a library of all games you’ve played, including ones you don’t currently own.

- **Microsoft Account Online**
  - Visit [account.microsoft.com](https://account.microsoft.com) and sign in.
  - Go to **Xbox Profile** → **Achievements**.
  - This page lists every game where you’ve unlocked at least one achievement. Even if you don’t own it anymore, it will still appear.

- **TrueAchievements / Third-Party Trackers**
  - Sites like **TrueAchievements.com** sync with your Xbox account.
  - They give you a **chronological list of every game you’ve ever played**, including achievement details and playtime.

---

### 🔍 Tips for Finding That Forgotten Game
- Look at your **Achievements list** — even if you only played for 10 minutes, it’ll be logged.
- Check your **Game Pass history** if you used it; many forgotten titles come from there.
- If you remember **genre, art style, or mechanics**, you can filter your library by category to jog your memory.
