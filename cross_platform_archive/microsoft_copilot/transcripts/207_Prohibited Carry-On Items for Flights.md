**You cannot bring items like firearms, explosives, flammable substances, sharp objects, and large liquids in your carry-on bag. Many sports gear and tools are also banned.**

Here’s a detailed breakdown of *prohibited items in carry-on luggage* according to TSA guidelines:

---

### 🔫 **Weapons and Firearms**
- Firearms (including replicas and BB guns)
- Ammunition
- Gun parts (including magazines and firing pins)
- Starter pistols
- Toy weapons that resemble real firearms

### 💣 **Explosives and Flammable Items**
- Fireworks
- Gunpowder
- Flammable paints
- Gasoline, lighter fluid
- Aerosol insecticides (unless labeled non-HAZMAT and packed in checked baggage)
- Strike-anywhere matches

### 🔪 **Sharp Objects**
- Knives (including pocket knives and multi-tools with blades)
- Box cutters
- Razor blades (non-safety types)
- Swords and sabers
- Ice axes and picks
- Meat cleavers

*Note: Small scissors (under 4 inches) and nail clippers are allowed.*

### 🧪 **Chemicals and Toxic Substances**
- Bleach
- Spray paint
- Chlorine
- Tear gas
- Fertilizers
- Peroxides

### 🧯 **Self-Defense Items**
- Pepper spray and mace
- Stun guns and tasers
- Brass knuckles
- Martial arts weapons

### 🏏 **Sporting Equipment**
- Baseball bats
- Ski poles
- Golf clubs
- Hockey sticks
- Pool cues
- Bows and arrows

*These must go in checked baggage.*

### 🧴 **Liquids, Gels, and Aerosols**
- Any container over 3.4 ounces (100 ml) is prohibited unless exempt (e.g., baby formula, medications)
- Must fit in a single quart-sized zip-top bag

### 🧰 **Tools**
- Crowbars
- Drills and drill bits
- Hammers
- Saws
- Wrenches and pliers over 7 inches
- Screwdrivers over 7 inches

### 🧨 **Other Unusual Items**
- Hoverboards (depending on airline)
- Air mattresses with built-in pumps (check airline size rules)
- E-cigarettes and vaping devices (must be carried on, not checked)

---

For the most complete and up-to-date list, you can explore the TSA’s official [“What Can I Bring?” page](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/whatcanibring/all).

You cannot bring a butane torch lighter in your carry-on bag. 🔥

According to TSA regulations, **torch lighters**—which produce a hot, concentrated flame—are **strictly prohibited** in both carry-on and checked baggage. This includes:

- Butane torch lighters
- Jet flame lighters
- Blue flame lighters
- Any lighter used for cigars or culinary torching

These are considered **hazardous materials** due to their high heat output and pressurized fuel. Even if the lighter is empty, it’s still banned because of its design.

✅ **Allowed lighter types in carry-on (with restrictions):**
- Standard disposable lighters (e.g., Bic)
- Zippo-style lighters (without fuel in checked bags)
- One lighter per passenger
