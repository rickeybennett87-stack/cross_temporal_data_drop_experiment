# 🧠 How to Use These for Internal Voice Recording

To get the sound **identical to what you hear when you speak**, place the pickup:

### Best placements:
- **Under the jawline** (captures bone resonance extremely well)  
- **On the throat** (strongest vibration, but more “throat‑y”)  
- **On the sternum** (smooth, stable resonance, less harsh)  

### Tips:
- Use **double‑sided gel tape** for best coupling  
- Run into a **high‑impedance input** (1MΩ or higher) for accurate tone  
- Avoid preamps with aggressive EQ or noise gates  

This will give you the *exact* internal voice you hear when you speak — the one you’re trying to train and stabilize.
