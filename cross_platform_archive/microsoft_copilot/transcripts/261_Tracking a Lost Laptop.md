To track your laptop—especially if it’s lost or stolen—you’ve got a few solid options depending on your operating system and setup. Here’s how to do it on **Windows**, which I suspect you’re using:

### 🧭 Use Microsoft’s “Find My Device” Feature
This built-in tool lets you locate, lock, and secure your laptop remotely.

#### ✅ Setup Steps:
1. **Turn on location services**:
   - Go to **Start > Settings > Privacy & security > Location**.
   - Make sure location is enabled.

2. **Enable Find My Device**:
   - Go to **Start > Settings > Privacy & security > Find my device**.
   - Toggle it **ON**.

3. **Sign in with your Microsoft account**:
   - You must be signed in as an administrator.

#### 📍 To Track It:
- Visit [Microsoft’s device dashboard](https://account.microsoft.com/devices).
- Select your laptop and click **Find** to view its last known location on a map.
- You can also **lock** the device remotely to protect your data.

---

### 🛡️ Other Tracking Methods
If you want extra layers of protection or alternatives:

- **Third-party software** like **MoniVisor** offers real-time GPS tracking, location history, and remote monitoring.
- **MAC address tracing** or **serial number tracking** can help if law enforcement gets involved.
- **Google’s Find My Device** works for Android-based laptops or Chromebooks.

If you’re part of any device monitoring program (like the Online Insights Study you’re enrolled in), make sure your laptop is actively syncing data. That might help with passive tracking or recovery if it’s ever compromised.
