### 🧪 Step-by-Step: Getting Tarasque Ichor

1. **Head to Containment Block ANNING**

3. **Slice the Carcass**  
   - After defeating the Tarasque, interact with its body to collect the **Tarasque Ichor**.

---

### 🌌 Unlocking the Mycofields Portal

1. **Return to Portal Lab 04**  
   - Ride the elevator back up and head to the lab where fungal growth is visible.

2. **Use the Ichor**  
   - Insert the Tarasque Ichor into the **Flow Control Module** to activate the portal.

3. **Hack the Keypad**  
   - You’ll need **Class 3 Clearance** to access the portal. Once hacked, the gateway to the Mycofields opens.
