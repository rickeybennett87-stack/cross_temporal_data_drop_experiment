### 🌐 What ResearchGate Is
- **Founded in 2008**, ResearchGate is a **commercial social networking site for scientists and researchers**.  
- It has **25+ million users worldwide**, primarily academics in medicine, biology, engineering, computer science, and more.  
- The platform lets members **upload research outputs** (papers, data, chapters, patents, methods, presentations, software code), ask questions, and find collaborators.  
- Think of it as a mashup of **Facebook, LinkedIn, and Google Scholar**, but focused entirely on research.  

---

### 📄 What a Preprint Is
- A **preprint** is your **original draft version of a paper before peer review or journal publication**.  
- Commonly, preprints are **articles, book chapters, or conference papers/posters** shared early.  
- Benefits of uploading preprints:  
  - Gain **early visibility** and feedback from peers.  
  - Build an audience before formal publication.  
  - Get a **free DOI (Digital Object Identifier)** for your preprint, ensuring credit and easy citation.  
  - Link your preprint to the final published version later.  

---

### 🛠️ How to Upload a Preprint on ResearchGate
1. **Create a Profile**  
   - Sign up at [researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net).  
   - Verify your email (usually institutional or manually confirmed as a published researcher).  

2. **Add Your Preprint**  
   - Go to your profile and click **“Add new” → “Preprint”**.  
   - Enter details: title, authors, abstract, keywords, etc.  
   - Upload your draft file (commonly a .doc or PDF).  
   - Choose whether to make the file **public or private**.  

3. **Check Publisher Policies**  
   - Some publishers allow preprints freely, others impose restrictions.  
   - Always confirm your **copyright/licensing agreement** before uploading.  

4. **Link to Published Version Later**  
   - Once your paper is published, go to your preprint entry.  
   - Select **“Add published version”** to connect it to the final article.  

---

### ⚠️ Things to Keep in Mind

- **Visibility:** Preprints are indexed and can be cited, so they help establish your research record early.  
- **Community:** Engaging with comments and recommendations on your preprint can spark collaborations.  

---

**In short:** ResearchGate is a global hub for researchers. To upload a preprint, set up your profile, add the draft under “Preprint,” and ensure you comply with publisher rules. This way, your work gains visibility and feedback even before formal publication.
