## 🧠 What Is PEMDAS?

PEMDAS is a mnemonic for the **order in which operations are evaluated** in math and programming:

| **Letter** | **Operation**         | **Example**              |
|------------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| P          | Parentheses            | \((2 + 3) \times 4 = 20\) |
| E          | Exponents              | \(2^3 = 8\)               |
| M          | Multiplication         | \(3 \times 4 = 12\)       |
| D          | Division               | \(12 \div 3 = 4\)         |
| A          | Addition               | \(5 + 2 = 7\)             |
| S          | Subtraction            | \(9 - 4 = 5\)             |

**Multiplication and Division** are evaluated **left to right**, same with **Addition and Subtraction**.

3. **Multiplication**: \(2 \times 8 = 16\)
