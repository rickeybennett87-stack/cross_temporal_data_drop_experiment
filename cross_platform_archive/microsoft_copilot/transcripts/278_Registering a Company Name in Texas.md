### 🛠️ Step-by-Step Guide to Registering a Company Name in Texas

#### 1. **Choose Your Business Structure**
- Decide whether you're forming an LLC, corporation, partnership, or sole proprietorship.
- The structure determines your filing requirements and protections.

#### 2. **Check Name Availability**
- Use the [Texas Secretary of State’s SOSDirect tool](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/do-business.shtml) to search for name availability.
- Your chosen name must be distinguishable from existing entities and comply with Texas naming rules.

#### 3. **File the Appropriate Formation Documents**
- For LLCs and corporations, file a Certificate of Formation with the Secretary of State.
- You can file online via [SOSDirect](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/do-business.shtml) or by mail.
- Include the name, registered agent, and other required details.

#### 4. **Register an Assumed Name (if needed)**
- If you're operating under a name different from your legal entity name, file an Assumed Name Certificate with the county clerk and/or the Secretary of State.
- This is especially common for sole proprietors and partnerships.

#### 5. **Pay Filing Fees**
- Fees vary by entity type. For example, forming an LLC typically costs around $300.
- Payment options include credit card, check, or money order payable to the Secretary of State.

#### 6. **Secure Your EIN (Employer Identification Number)**
- Apply for an EIN through the IRS website. This is essential for tax purposes and hiring employees.

#### 7. **Check for Licenses and Permits**
- Depending on your business type, you may need state or local licenses.
- Use the [Texas license directory](https://www.texas.gov/starting-business-texas/) to identify what's required.
