You can absolutely clear out that ghost connection. Here’s how to remove a registered computer from the Link to Windows app on your phone—even if the device is damaged or no longer running Windows:

### 🧹 Step-by-Step Cleanup

#### 1. **Remove the Device from Your Microsoft Account**
This ensures the old computer is no longer associated with your account:
- Go to [Microsoft’s Devices page](https://account.microsoft.com/devices)
- Sign in with your Microsoft account
- Find the damaged computer in the list
- Select **Remove device**

#### 2. **Clear the Link to Windows App on Your Phone**
This helps reset the app’s memory of old connections:
- On your phone, go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **Phone Link** (or **Link to Windows**)
- Tap **Storage** and then **Clear Cache** and **Clear Data**
- Restart your phone

#### 3. **Reconnect Only the New Device**
After clearing, open the Link to Windows app again and pair it with your current computer. This should eliminate the old one from showing up.

#### 4. **Optional: Check Device Management in the App**
Some versions of the app have a **Device Management** or **Settings** section where you can manually remove old devices. If available:
- Open the Link to Windows app
- Navigate to **Settings** or **Connected Devices**
- Tap the old computer and look for a **Remove** or **Forget** option
