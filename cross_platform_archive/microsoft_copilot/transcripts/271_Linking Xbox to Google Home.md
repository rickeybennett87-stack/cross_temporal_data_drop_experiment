To make sure your current Xbox is properly linked to Google Home—especially if your Microsoft account was previously tied to older consoles—you’ll need to refresh the connection and reassign the correct device. Here's how to do that step-by-step:

### 🛠 Microsoft & Xbox Setup

1. **Sign in to your Microsoft Account**
   - Visit [account.microsoft.com](https://account.microsoft.com) and confirm you're signed in with the same account used on your current Xbox.

2. **Check Xbox Console Association**
   - On your Xbox, go to **Settings > Account > Sign-in, security & passkey** and ensure the correct Microsoft account is signed in.
   - If needed, remove any old profiles and re-add your current one.

3. **Enable Digital Assistants**
   - On your Xbox, go to **Settings > Devices & connections > Digital assistants** and toggle it **on**. This allows Google Assistant to communicate with your console.

4. **Set Power Mode to Instant-On**
   - Navigate to **Settings > General > Power mode & startup** and choose **Instant-on**. This ensures your Xbox can respond to voice commands even when idle.

---

### 📱 Google Home App Linking

1. **Open the Google Home App**
   - Tap **+ Add > Set up device > Works with Google**.
   - Search for **Xbox** and select it.

2. **Link Microsoft Account**
   - Follow the prompts to sign in with your Microsoft account. This step re-establishes the connection between Google and your current Xbox.

3. **Assign Console to a Room**
   - Once linked, assign your Xbox to a room in the Google Home app. This helps with voice command targeting.

4. **Test Voice Commands**
   - Try saying: “Hey Google, turn on Xbox” or “Launch YouTube on Xbox.” Make sure you use the name of the console as it appears in the app.

---

If you’ve already linked your Microsoft account in the past, but the console has changed, this process ensures the new Xbox is recognized and controllable. You’re essentially re-binding the voice assistant to the correct hardware.
