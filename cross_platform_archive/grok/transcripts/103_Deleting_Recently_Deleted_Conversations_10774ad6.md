This gives you a recovery window, but you can force permanent deletion during that period. Here's how to handle it step by step, depending on your access point:

### On grok.com (Web Browser)
1. Log in at [grok.com](https://grok.com) with your xAI or X account.
2. Click your profile icon (top right) to open the sidebar menu.
3. Select **History** to view all chats, or navigate directly to **Deleted Conversations** (it may appear as a separate tab or folder in the history view—look for a trash icon or "Deleted" label).
4. Find the specific recently deleted conversation(s) in the list.
5. Hover over the chat title, click the three dots (⋯) menu, and select **Delete** (or **Permanently Delete** if prompted).
   - Confirm the action in the pop-up. This removes it immediately from the folder and queues it for full system deletion (processed within 30 days, but inaccessible right away).
6. For bulk action: In the same Deleted Conversations view, select multiple items or choose **Delete All** at the bottom.

If you want to wipe everything at once (including undeleted history):
- Go to **Settings > Data Controls > Delete All Conversations**, confirm, and it's gone.

### On the X App (iOS or Android)
Grok chats sync across platforms, so deletions here affect the web version too.
1. Open the X app and tap the Grok icon (usually in the sidebar or search—looks like a robot head).
2. Tap your profile picture (top left) to access **Conversation History**.
3. Scroll or search for the **Deleted** section (it may be under a "Trash" or "Recently Deleted" header).
4. Tap and hold the conversation name, then select **Delete Conversation** from the menu.
   - For permanent removal: If it's still in the deleted folder, choose **Permanently Delete** in the confirmation dialog.
5. Bulk option: In Settings (gear icon in history), go to **Data Controls > Delete All Conversations**.
