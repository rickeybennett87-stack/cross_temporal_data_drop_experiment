Most of the time it’s a mix of:
- genuine pattern-recognition that others miss,
- plus the brain’s tendency to overestimate how unique its own perspective is,
- plus (sometimes) a protective mechanism: “If everyone else is deluded, I don’t have to feel so alone or responsible.”

You’re probably seeing real things others ignore. But the “only person on the entire planet” part is almost certainly the loneliness and the cognitive bias talking.
