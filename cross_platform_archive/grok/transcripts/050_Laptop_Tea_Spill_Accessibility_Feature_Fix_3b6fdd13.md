It sounds like your laptop got some iced tea (or whatever tea) spilled on it, and now the “TalkBack” (or VoiceOver / screen reader) accessibility feature suddenly turned on — this is super common when liquid hits certain keys or the trackpad.

Here’s how to turn it off immediately, depending on what kind of laptop you have:

### Windows Laptop (most likely what you have)
The spill probably triggered “Narrator” or a sticky-keys shortcut.

1. Press **Ctrl + Windows key + Enter** at the same time.  
   This instantly turns Narrator off in Windows 10/11.

   (If that doesn’t work, try holding the Windows key and pressing Enter.)

2. Alternative way (if the first one doesn’t work right away):  
   - Press **Windows key + Ctrl + N** → this opens Narrator settings  
   - Then press Tab a few times until you hear “Exit Narrator” or “Turn off” and hit Space or Enter.

### MacBook
Spills often trigger VoiceOver.

1. Press **Command + F5** (or **Cmd + Fn + F5** on newer Macs) — this toggles VoiceOver on/off instantly.

   Or  
2. Hold down the Touch ID/power button for a second → you’ll hear “VoiceOver off” if it was on.

### Chromebook
1. Press **Ctrl + Alt + Z** — this turns TalkBack on/off.

### Right Now (Emergency Steps)
- Unplug the laptop and remove the battery if possible (most modern laptops don’t have removable batteries).
- Flip it upside down like a tent (keyboard facing down) so liquid drains out, not deeper in.
- Do NOT keep using it or try to charge it until it’s dried out (at least 48 hours, preferably with rice or silica packets or just good airflow).

Let me know if the voice is still talking after you try the shortcuts above, and tell me if it’s Windows, Mac, or Chromebook — I’ll give you the exact next steps so you don’t lose the machine!
