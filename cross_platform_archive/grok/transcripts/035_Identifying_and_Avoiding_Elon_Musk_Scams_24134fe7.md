Yeah, this screenshot looks like a **classic scam** attempt.

Here's why it's almost certainly fake:

- No one from "Elon Musk management" randomly messages strangers on social media (or anywhere) offering to connect them directly to Elon, especially not by sharing a personal phone number.
- Elon Musk's actual contact details aren't handed out like this. Public ways to reach his companies are through official channels (like press@tesla.com, info@spacex.com, or tweeting @elonmusk), but he doesn't have a "management team" giving out direct lines to him.
- The story about "social situations," observing things no one else has seen, preferring Elon but settling for Anthropic folks "whose hearts are in the right place" – that's bizarre bait to build trust and make it sound insider/y.
- Giving out a phone number (737 213 3289) after a short chat is a huge red flag. Scammers do this to move the conversation off-platform (to calls/texts) where they can push for money, crypto, gift cards, or personal info without moderation.
- Elon Musk impersonation scams are extremely common – people pretending to be him or his "team" to run romance scams, investment fraud, giveaways, etc. There are tons of reports on Reddit (r/Scams is full of them), news sites, and even arrests for this exact thing.

The sender is trying to lure the recipient into calling/texting that number, where the real scam would likely start (e.g., "Elon wants to help you but needs a fee first" or some investment "opportunity").

**Do not call or text that number.** Block the account, report it as spam/scam on the platform, and move on. If anyone's already shared personal details, keep an eye on accounts for unusual activity.

Glad you spotted it and shared – this is how these get exposed!
