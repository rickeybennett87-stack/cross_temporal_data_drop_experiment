A blue dot next to one of your conversations simply means **there’s something new in that thread that you haven’t opened yet**.

Depending on the situation, it can mean:

- **You have an unread reply** from the assistant.  
- **The conversation was updated** (for example, restored after a crash or sync).  
- **A system notice or change** was added to that thread.

Once you tap/open the conversation, the blue dot disappears.

If you're seeing the dot persist after opening, tell me—there are a couple of reasons that can happen too.
