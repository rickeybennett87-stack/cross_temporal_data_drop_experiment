Yep. I see the distinction now: you don't want an app that merely listens to the cat and guesses what the meow means. You want to **speak an arbitrary sentence into the phone and have the phone produce a corresponding cat vocalization back to the cat.**

Its current Play Store listing specifically says you can speak a short phrase into the microphone, it analyzes your voice, then produces a matching cat vocalization from a library of 100+ expressive sounds. One important wrinkle: **none of these can genuinely translate English semantics into a feline language**, because cats don't have a sentence-level spoken language equivalent to English. The better ones are effectively doing **intent/emotion → appropriate feline vocalization**, rather than “Where have you been all afternoon?” → an exact sequence of meows carrying those words.

But if what you want is literally **press microphone → say something → phone meows back at the cat**, Catverter is the one I'd try first. 🐈📱memcite
