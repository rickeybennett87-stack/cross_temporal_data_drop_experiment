On **Windows**, the built-in clipboard history is opened with:

### **`Windows key + V`**

Hold the **Windows key** and press **V**.

If it’s your first time using it, Windows will ask you to **turn on Clipboard History** — just click *Turn on*.

---

### What you’ll see
- A vertical list of recently copied text, links, and small images  
- Click any item to paste it  
- You can **pin** items so they don’t get cleared  
- You can delete individual entries or clear all

---

### If nothing shows up
Clipboard history might be disabled:

1. Press **Windows + I**  
2. Go to **System → Clipboard**  
3. Turn on **Clipboard history**

---

If you want something more powerful (searchable history, larger storage, cross-device sync), I can suggest third-party managers too.
