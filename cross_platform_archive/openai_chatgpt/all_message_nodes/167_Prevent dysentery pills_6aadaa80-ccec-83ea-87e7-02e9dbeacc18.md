There is something that can reduce the risk, but there isn't a pill that reliably prevents dysentery.

For travelers, **bismuth subsalicylate** (the active ingredient in Pepto-Bismol) is the main non-antibiotic medication studied for prevention. CDC reports that it can reduce travelers' diarrhea by roughly **50%**, though it isn't convenient for long-term use and isn't safe for everyone. citeturn0search0turn0search1

Preventive antibiotics can reduce bacterial diarrhea substantially, but CDC **doesn't recommend them routinely** because of antibiotic resistance, C. difficile infection, side effects, and disruption of normal gut bacteria. Importantly, actual **dysentery**, meaning diarrhea with visible blood, is treated as severe illness rather than ordinary diarrhea.
