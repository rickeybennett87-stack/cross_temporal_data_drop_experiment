Gemini Live Description and Capabilities

Gemini Live allows a user to have a natural

conversation with Al while sharing what the user sees

through a camera or screen. It allows for multimodal
interaction where Al can understand and discuss the

world around a user. 2

Key capabilities include:

e Verbal interaction: Users can talk to Al without
typing, and Al responds verbally.

e Real-time camera sharing: Users can point a
phone's camera at objects or surroundings to get
instant information or help, such as identifying
architecture, comparing products while shopping, or
understanding a complex DIY project.

e Screen sharing ability: This allows Al to see and
understand the content on a phone's screen in real
time. This is useful for:

o Getting advice on social media posts.
© Getting ideas and recommendations for gifts while
browsing websites.
o Asking questions about the content on a webpage
